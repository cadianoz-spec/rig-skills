# Australian Engineering Guardrails

## Core Guidelines
1. **Fire Sprinkler Systems (AS 2118.1 & AS 4118.2.1):** 
   - Verify Hazen-Williams friction loss formulas using appropriate C-values (e.g., C = 120 for Galvanised/Black Steel).
   - Ensure proper unit conversions ($L/s$ to $m^3/s$, $mm$ to $m$).
   - Check that suction runs minimize single points of failure, maintain adequate static head to prevent cavitation, and keep outlet spacing compliant ($\ge 5.0\text{ m}$).
2. **Plumbing & Drainage (AS/NZS 3500):**
   - Ensure pipe sizing, gradients, and venting comply with national plumbing codes.
3. **Review Methodology:**
   - Highlight critical spatial clashes, road reserve encroachments, and hose relay friction losses when evaluating CAD floor plan layouts.
